Hi, I'm Behjat.

I build multi-agent AI systems and the careful infrastructure around them — retrieval pipelines, evaluation harnesses, regression tests. The parts that separate a demo from a system people can trust.

I'm finishing my MS in Computer Science at Emory University (GPA 3.945, December 2026). I hold two research assistant positions at once — one in an HCI lab running a 150+ paper systematic review, one at the School of Nursing building a clinical imaging pipeline.

My first real NLP project had no dataset, so I built the 100K+ word corpus first, then the model. That instinct — build the missing foundation, then the thing — is still how I work.

Also: 4 Anthropic certifications (Claude SDK, MCP, Claude Code, AI Fluency). I like knowing my tools all the way down.
